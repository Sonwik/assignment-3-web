    FAQ

    New to fixed gear?

    The rear cog is screwed to the hub without a freewheel. When the wheel turns, the pedals turn too — you cannot coast. It feels very direct: you control the speed with your legs.

    You resist the pedals to slow down, or you do a skid: move your weight forward, lock your legs and the rear wheel slides. We still recommend a front brake for city traffic — every build can get one for free.

    For the city, most riders use 2.6–3.0 (for example 48×17). Lighter gears are better for tricks and hills, heavier ones for speed. Try different gears in our gear calculator.

    A single speed has one gear too, but with a freewheel, so you can stop pedaling. Our Intro7 wheels have a flip-flop hub: a fixed cog on one side and a freewheel on the other.
