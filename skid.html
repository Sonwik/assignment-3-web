Skid Challenge

A fixie has no brakes — you stop with a skid. Hold the button (or Space) to lock the rear wheel, let go to roll again. Stop your front wheel as close to the line as you can. 5 rounds, every round is faster.

    How it works

    • Holding = the rear wheel is locked and the bike slows down. Not holding = it rolls at the same speed.
    • Score: 100 points if the front wheel stops right at the line, minus 1 point for every 2 cm before it.
    • Crossed the line or rode off the road — 0 points for the round.
    • Faster bike = longer skid: start braking earlier in later rounds.
